 ## AI Job Finder

An AI-powered job recommendation automation built with n8n, Airtable, Jobs API, AI, and Email.


## Project Overview

AI Job Finder helps users discover relevant job opportunities based on their preferred job role and country.

A user submits:

- Name
- Email
- Preferred Job Role
- Country

The automation processes the information, searches for available job opportunities, uses AI to identify the most relevant opportunities, and sends personalized job recommendations to the user.

## Workflow
![AI Job Finder](./AI-job-finder-workflow.png)

User Form → Airtable → Jobs API → AI Recommendation → Airtable → Email

## Workflow Steps

1. User submits their job preferences through a form.
2. The applicant's information is saved in Airtable.
3. The workflow searches for available jobs.
4. Job opportunities are sent to the AI for analysis.
5. The AI recommends the 3 most relevant opportunities.
6. The recommendations are stored in Airtable.
7. The user receives the recommendations by email.

## Tools Used

- n8n — Workflow automation
- Airtable — Data storage
- Jobs API / job data — Job opportunity search
- AI — Job matching and recommendations
- Email — Delivery of personalized recommendations

## Objective

To automate the process of finding and recommending relevant job opportunities while reducing the amount of manual job searching required by users.
